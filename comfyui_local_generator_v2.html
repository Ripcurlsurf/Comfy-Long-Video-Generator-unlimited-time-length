Suggested settings


Browsers cannot read your exact VRAM or RAM. Detect GPU reads the graphics card name and fills in its usual VRAM; confirm both values (nvidia-smi shows VRAM). Suggestions update as you change the video model, image model, goal, RAM, VRAM or scenes.


Scenes


Reference images


 
















Angles are generated from each reference's original image with Qwen Image 2.1 or Flux 2, so every angle shows the same person or place. Z-Image Turbo and MiniMax H3 cannot do this: give those references your own angle images on the reference card. Untick "Create extra angles" on any reference that does not need them. Set each scene's camera view in the scene list, or leave it on Auto.
