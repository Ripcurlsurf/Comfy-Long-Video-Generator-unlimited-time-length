Video and image engines

Unzip it into ComfyUI/custom_nodes/ (you get ComfyUI/custom_nodes/ripcurlsurf_comfy/refimagetools.py) and restart ComfyUI. It is plain image processing (numpy), no extra models or installs.

Quality pipeline: draft, takes, finishing and audio

                    The route to an hour-long film that holds quality: render a fast draft
                    of everything, approve it, re-render only the good shots at full quality (several takes if you
                    like), then finish everything with one grade and one final encode. Every setting here is saved in
                    templates and projects.
